# Imagery

Reference photos from the guidelines' logo-on-photo examples.

- `field-distribution-light.jpg`: a light, open sky over an aid distribution. Correct pairing: the coloured logo in the empty sky. Wrong: the white logo here.
- `leaf-high-contrast-dark.jpg`: a dark, calm crop of a bean plant. Correct pairing: the white logo over the dark area.

Never place a logo on a busy, crowded photo, such as a classroom full of children.
