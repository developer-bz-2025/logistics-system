# Logos

Official lockups, traced from the brand guidelines as vector SVG. Colours are fixed in the files: burgundy mark #6f1621 and olive wordmark #30602f (the PDF's own values), or all white.

- `bz-logo-horizontal-color.svg`: mark left of wordmark, coloured. White or very light grounds only.
- `bz-logo-vertical-color.svg`: mark above wordmark, coloured. White or very light grounds only.
- `bz-logo-horizontal-white.svg`: white. Burgundy, charcoal or dark, neat photos.
- `bz-logo-vertical-white.svg`: white. Burgundy, charcoal or dark, neat photos.
- `bz-mark-burgundy.svg`: the calligraphic mark alone, burgundy. For avatars, favicons, stamps on light grounds.
- `bz-mark-white.svg`: the mark alone, white, for dark grounds.

Always place the logo on the left of a layout. Never recolour, stretch or redraw it.
