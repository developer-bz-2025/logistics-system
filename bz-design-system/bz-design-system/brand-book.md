Basmeh & Zeitooneh is a refugee-led relief and development organization, founded in Lebanon in 2012 by volunteers responding to the needs of Syrian refugees. Vision: *A Constructive Society that Lives with Dignity.* Every output should look consistent and professional, because that consistency is what carries the trust the organization has built with communities, partners and supporters.

## The name

- Always write **Basmeh & Zeitooneh**, with an ampersand. Never "Basmeh and Zeitooneh".
- The short form is **B&Z** (no spaces).
- The descriptor is **Relief & Development**, set in uppercase in the logo lockup.
- Arabic name: بسمة وزيتونة.

## Content fundamentals

The tone was created to be one voice for Syrian refugees across all nations and circumstances. It reflects three values: **empathy, respect and empowerment**.

- Be open, honest, factual and transparent. The facts can be overwhelming; deliver them in a way that is engaging, positive and enabling.
- Carry a confident, can-do urgency: an invitation to act, collaborate and make an impact that matters.
- Use clear, inclusive, positive language. Avoid jargon and technical terms not every audience will understand.
- Speak as "we" for the organization. Say "participants" and "communities". Interventions are *co-designed with* participants, never done *to* them.
- "Founded by refugees, for refugees" is the defining line. Use it when the audience needs to know who B&Z is.
- Headings are short nouns in title case ("Vision", "Mission", "About B&Z"). Body copy is sentence case.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks in formal material.

## Visual foundations

### Colour

- `burgundy` (#6f1921) is the brand. Use it for the logo mark, headings on light grounds (`heading`), primary actions and full-bleed panels (`surface-brand`).
- The primary palette also includes `grey-100`, `blush` and `mauve`: soft grounds and dividers. Use `surface-alt` for alternate bands and `surface-tint` for soft branded panels.
- The secondary palette (`sun`, `sky`, `olive`, `mint`, `coral`, `orchid`) is for charts, infographics and illustration accents. It never replaces burgundy as the lead colour. `olive` is also the wordmark green of the coloured logo.
- Text is black or dark grey: `ink` (#000000) for primary text and `ink-soft` (#4d4b4d) for secondary and long body text. On burgundy, text is `on-brand` (white) or `on-brand-soft` (blush).
- Contrast: `ink-faint` (#7f7f7f, the guidelines' caption grey) is 4.0:1 on white. It is kept exact, so limit it to non-essential captions at 13px and up. Use `ink-soft` for anything a reader must read. `sun`, `mint` and `orchid` carry black text only. `sky` and `coral` carry white text only at 24px and up.
- Dark theme: `surface` goes near-black (#1c1a1b), `heading` becomes `mauve`, and `surface-brand` stays burgundy. Use the white logo in dark theme.
- Interaction: links are `link` (burgundy) and hover to `link-hover`. Burgundy buttons hover to `burgundy-dark` (#531419). Selected text is white on `selection`.
- News and card titles use `heading-secondary` (olive), as on the website. Section headings stay `heading` (burgundy).
- Focus: `focus-ring` is a 2px solid ring with a 2px gap in the page colour, at 3:1 or better on every surface.

### Typography

- English display: **Helvetica Neue Condensed Bold** (`display`, `title`, `label`) for titles, covers and the wordmark treatment.
- English text: **Helvetica Neue**, mostly the Light weight (`heading`, `lead`, `body`, `caption`).
- Secondary English fonts: **Helvetica**, then **Arial**, when Helvetica Neue isn't available (Word, Google Docs, email).
- Arabic: **DIN Next LT Arabic** (`arabic-heading`, `arabic-body`). Always set `dir="rtl"`.
- The brand fonts are licensed and not bundled here. The stacks fall back to Roboto Condensed / Arial Narrow for display and Noto Kufi Arabic / Tahoma for Arabic. Install the real fonts from the organization's font pack for print and design work.
- Body text has a line height of 1.65. Button labels use `button` (15px, 600), and hero buttons use `button-caps` (12px, 600, uppercase). Card titles use `card-title` (20px, 600).
- Headings sit in `heading` colour at regular weight, not bold. Titles are condensed bold. Don't set body text in the condensed face.

### Layout and shape

- Panels, images and bands have square corners (`radius-none`). Buttons and form fields use `radius-sm` (3px), as on the website. Hero-slider buttons use `radius-slider` (15px). The only circles are colour-swatch discs (`radius-full`).
- Pages alternate between full-bleed burgundy panels (covers, section openers, closing pages) and white content pages.
- Content pages carry a **left rail**: a thin vertical hairline broken by the section name, rotated 90° and set in `rail` (see the SectionRail component).
- Side notes sit beside content with a thin vertical rule to their left, in `caption` (see the Note component).
- Generous margins: `space-12` on slides and wide layouts, `space-16` of clear space around the logo on covers.
- Keep standard content within `container` (1290px), and articles within `container-narrow` (750px). Leave `space-15` (60px) between page sections.
- No shadows and no gradients. Separate things with space, hairlines (`line`) and flat colour panels.

### Logo

- Two lockups: **vertical** (mark above the wordmark) and **horizontal** (mark left of the wordmark). Use whichever suits the space.
- **Coloured logo** (burgundy mark, olive wordmark): only on white or very light grounds.
- **White logo**: on burgundy, on `charcoal`, or on dark, uncluttered photos.
- **Placement**: the B&Z logo always goes on the **left** of a layout. When a partner's logo is featured, adjust placement to follow the partner's guidelines.
- Don't place the white logo on a light photo. Don't place any logo on a busy, crowded photo. Don't recolour, stretch or redraw the mark.

### Imagery

- Photograph real work and real people with dignity: distributions, classrooms, livelihoods, agriculture.
- For a logo on a photo, choose a calm area with high contrast. Use the coloured logo on light, clean areas and the white logo on dark, neat areas. See `assets/Imagery`.

## Iconography

The guidelines define no icon set. Use a simple line icon family with a consistent stroke, in `ink` or `burgundy`. Never use emoji as icons.

## Filled in from the website

The PDF guidelines leave some values open. These were measured on basmeh-zeitooneh.org on 2026-09-29 and added to the system:

- Colours: `burgundy-dark` #531419 for hover, and `grey-dark` #363336. Both are in the website's theme palette but not in the PDF. The other 13 website colours match the guidelines exactly.
- Interaction: link and link-hover colours, selection colour, and form-field fill and border (`input-bg`, `input-border`).
- Shape: 3px corners on buttons and fields (`radius-sm`) and 15px on hero buttons (`radius-slider`).
- Sizes: button padding of 12px × 24px, the compact Donate button at 5px × 20px, a 40px field height, 1290px and 750px container widths, and 60px section spacing.
- Type: body line height 1.65, button label styles, and olive card titles.

Where the website differs from the guidelines, the guidelines win:

- The website asks for **Myriad Pro** for text but never loads it, and uses **Times New Roman** for headings and **Arial** for buttons. The guidelines specify Helvetica Neue and Helvetica Neue Condensed Bold, so the system keeps those. The website should be updated to match.
- The website's **page background** is `grey-100` (#efefef). The system keeps white `surface` as the default and uses `surface-alt` for grey bands.

Verified against the live site on 2026-09-29. Measured: buttons (primary, compact Donate, hero slider), the newsletter field, links, news-card titles, nav and body text. Not measured: dropdown menus, the footer and inner pages.
