# SectionRail

The left-edge rail found on every B&Z content page: a thin vertical hairline broken by the section name, rotated to read bottom to top. The consumer provides `label` (the section name, e.g. "Color Palette") and the page content as children. Headings inside use the `heading` colour and regular weight.

- One rail per page or slide, always on the left.
- Keep the label to one to three words, in title case.
