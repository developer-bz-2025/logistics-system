# Logo

Renders the official Basmeh & Zeitooneh lockup chosen by layout and ground. `layout` is `horizontal` (default), `vertical` or `mark`; `on="light"` gives the coloured logo (burgundy mark, olive wordmark) and `on="dark"` the white logo. The consumer provides the container and, optionally, `height`.

- Coloured logo only on white or very light grounds. White logo on burgundy, `charcoal` or dark, uncluttered photos.
- Place it on the left of the layout, unless a partner's guidelines say otherwise.
- Never recolour, stretch, outline or redraw it, and never set it on a busy photo.
- Outside this system, pass `src` with your own hosted copy of the files in `assets/Logos`.
